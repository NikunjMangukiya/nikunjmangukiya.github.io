About

Background

I am a Postdoctoral Research Fellow at the National University of Singapore (NUS). I completed my Ph.D. in Hydrology at the Department of Hydrology, IIT Roorkee, where my research focused on developing deep learning-based frameworks for hydrological predictions — addressing data sparsity, temporally discrete observations, physical interpretability in regulated river systems, and flood risk assessment in ungauged watersheds.

My current work continues to explore the integration of artificial intelligence and hydrological sciences, with a focus on data-driven and physics-informed modeling, climate change impacts, and flood hazard mapping. I'm especially interested in fostering interdisciplinary collaborations to solve real-world water challenges.

My toolbox includes Python, R, MATLAB, MIKE Hydro River/Flood/11/21, HEC-RAS, ArcGIS, QGIS, and Google Earth Engine — alongside a continuing habit of chasing math puzzles for fun.
