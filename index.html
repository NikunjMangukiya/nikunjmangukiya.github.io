About

Background

I am a Postdoctoral Research Fellow at the National University of Singapore (NUS), Singapore. I recently completed my Ph.D. in Hydrology from the Department of Hydrology, Indian Institute of Technology Roorkee, where my research focused on developing deep learning-based frameworks for hydrological predictions. My work addressed key challenges such as data sparsity, temporally discrete observations, physical interpretability in regulated river systems, and flood risk assessment in ungauged watersheds.

My current research continues to explore the integration of artificial intelligence and hydrological sciences, with a particular focus on data-driven and physics-informed modeling, climate change impacts, and flood hazard mapping. I am deeply passionate about advancing scientific understanding and fostering interdisciplinary collaborations to solve real-world water challenges.

My academic journey has been shaped by valuable experiences. I completed my post-graduation (M.Tech.) degree in Water Resources Engineering from Sardar Vallabhbhai National Institute of Technology (SVNIT) in 2019, following my undergraduate (B.E.) degree in Civil Engineering from Gujarat Technological University (GTU) in 2016. These educational milestones have provided me with a strong foundation and sparked my curiosity to delve deeper into the complexities of water systems.

Life at an Institute of National Importance has been both enriching and challenging. Interacting with diverse groups of people and engaging in various research projects has taught me to be adaptable and open to new perspectives. I am grateful for the learning opportunities and chance to grow both professionally and personally.

While my toolbox of skills includes Python, MATLAB, HEC-RAS, MIKE+, ArcGIS, QGIS, MS Office, Strategic Planning, and Public Speaking, I am far from being a master in all of them. I am continuously learning and improving, finding joy in tackling math puzzles and aptitude problems, as well as staying organized to remain productive.

Above all, I am a firm believer in the power of passion and purpose. My experiences have given me a sense of direction, and I am eager to apply my knowledge and skills in real-world scenarios to make a positive impact in the field of Hydrology and Water Resources. I am always open to exciting opportunities that align with my interests and values, and I look forward to what the future holds in this thrilling and ever-evolving journey.

Let's embark on this fascinating exploration together, as we navigate the wonders of water and strive to create sustainable solutions for the challenges ahead!
